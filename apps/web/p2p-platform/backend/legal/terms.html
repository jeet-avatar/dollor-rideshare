WHAT DOLLOR.AI IS

Dollor.ai is a peer-to-peer rideshare marketplace for California. Riders post a ride offer and independent drivers bid on it. Dollor.ai provides the technology that connects them and processes payment. Dollor.ai does not own vehicles and does not employ drivers. Drivers are independent contractors.

Dollor.ai's application for a California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) Transportation Network Company (TNC) permit is under CPUC review. This page does not state that a permit has been issued.

3. How a Ride Works

  1. The Rider enters a pickup and destination and posts a ride request with an offer. A fare estimate and fee breakdown are shown before the request is posted.
  2. The request stays open for bids for 5 minutes. Drivers who are online and nearby can submit bids.
  3. The Rider may accept a bid, make a counter-offer, or decline. A request that is not accepted within the bidding window expires.
  4. When a bid is accepted (the "match"), the Rider's card is authorized through Stripe for the agreed fare. The card is authorized, not charged, at this point.
  5. The Driver picks up the Rider and completes the trip. When the trip is completed, the authorized amount is captured. Riders may add an optional tip after the trip.

If the card cannot be authorized at the match, the ride is cancelled and no ride takes place.

Pricing note: Suggested fares include a time-of-day adjustment (for example, higher at morning and evening peak hours and lower in the early afternoon). Any adjustment is shown in the fare estimate before you post a request. Dollor.ai does not guarantee that fares will stay constant across the day.

6.2 Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card is authorized when your ride is matched and the fare is captured when the ride is completed. Dollor.ai does not store full card numbers. Drivers are paid out through Stripe.

6.3 Cancellations

  • Rider cancels before the ride starts (before or after a driver is matched): there is no cancellation fee. The card authorization is released and open bids are closed.
  • Rider cannot cancel once the trip is in progress.
  • Driver cancels after a match: there is no charge to the Rider. The card authorization is released and the ride request is re-opened for other drivers. Frequent cancellations by a Driver may affect that Driver's standing.
  • Rider no-show: after the Driver has arrived at pickup and waited at least 5 minutes, the Driver may mark the Rider as a no-show. The ride is cancelled and the Rider's saved card is charged a $5.00 no-show fee, of which 80% ($4.00) is paid to the Driver.
  • Rider payment failure at the match: the ride is cancelled and no charge is made for the ride.

For refund or charge questions, contact support@dollor.ai with your trip ID.

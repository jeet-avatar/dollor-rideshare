PRIVACY COMMITMENT

Dollor.ai is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with California law. Dollor.ai is a peer-to-peer rideshare marketplace operated by Zietra Technologies Inc. We collect the data necessary to match riders with drivers and to provide rides in California.

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties. We do NOT share your information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

🏛️ California Residents — Your Rights at a Glance

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), you have:

  • Right to Know — what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Right to Delete — request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Correct — fix inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out — opt out of sale or sharing of personal information (we don't sell, but you can still opt out of any future sharing)
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information — including precise geolocation collected during trips
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — we will not penalize you for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any right: Email privacy@dollor.ai. To delete your account, use Profile → Delete Account in the app, or see www.dollor.ai/delete-account.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Zietra Technologies Inc., doing business as Dollor.ai ("Dollor.ai," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile applications, website, and related services (the "Platform").

This policy applies to:

  • Riders (customers who request transportation)
  • Driver-Partners (independent contractors who provide transportation)
  • All California residents who interact with the Platform

2.2 Sensitive Personal Information (CPRA)

Under the California Privacy Rights Act, the following categories of data we collect are classified as Sensitive Personal Information:

  • Precise Geolocation — Rider apps collect your precise location while the app is in use, to set your pickup point and show nearby drivers and your ride on the map. Driver apps collect precise location while the driver is online (on iOS this includes the background while online). Rider apps do not collect location when the app is not in use.
  • Government ID — Driver's license numbers collected for driver verification
  • Financial Account Information — Tokenized payment method details (processed by Stripe)

You have the right to limit our use of sensitive personal information to what is necessary to provide our transportation services. Contact privacy@dollor.ai to exercise this right.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

🏛️ Your California Privacy Rights — Full Detail

6.1 Right to Know (Cal. Civil Code § 1798.110)

You may request disclosure of:

  • The categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you
  • The categories of sources from which information was collected
  • The business or commercial purpose for collecting or sharing information
  • The categories of third parties with whom we share information

6.2 Right to Delete (Cal. Civil Code § 1798.105)

You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions (e.g., records required by California law for TNC compliance, fraud prevention).

6.3 Right to Correct (Cal. Civil Code § 1798.106 — CPRA)

You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we hold about you.

6.4 Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing (Cal. Civil Code § 1798.120)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You may still submit a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" request at any time by contacting privacy@dollor.ai.

6.5 Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information (CPRA)

You may request that we limit our use of your sensitive personal information (precise geolocation, government ID, payment account information) to only what is necessary to provide our transportation services. Note: limiting precise geolocation will impair core ride-matching functionality.

6.6 Right to Non-Discrimination (Cal. Civil Code § 1798.125)

We will not deny service, charge different prices, or provide lower quality service because you exercised your California privacy rights.

6.7 How to Submit a Request

  • Email: privacy@dollor.ai (include "California Privacy Request" in subject line)

We will verify your identity before processing requests. We respond within 45 days (extendable by 45 additional days with notice).

6.8 California Shine the Light (Civil Code § 1798.83)

California residents may request information about disclosure of personal information to third parties for direct marketing purposes. We do not disclose personal information for direct marketing by third parties.

6.9 California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA)

We honor "Do Not Track" signals from browsers for our website. Our mobile app does not track users across third-party apps or websites.

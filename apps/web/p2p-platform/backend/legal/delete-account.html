Delete Your Account and Data

Applies to: the Dollor rider app and the Dollor driver app (iOS and Android)
Operator: Zietra Technologies Inc. (doing business as Dollor.ai)
Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

Rider app

  1. Open the Dollor rider app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete Account. (You can also use Settings > Delete Account.)
  3. Confirm when asked. Your account is deleted and you are signed out.

Driver app

  1. Open the Dollor driver app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm when asked. Your account is deleted and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in or no longer have the app, email support@dollor.ai with the subject "Account deletion request" from the email address registered on your account. Tell us whether it is a rider or driver account. We may reply to confirm it is you. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your account record (name, email, phone number, password, profile and saved details, saved payment method references, and for drivers the vehicle and document details on your account)Removed or anonymised when your account is deleted: your name becomes "Deleted User", your email, phone number, addresses, photos, vehicle and document details are removed, and you can no longer sign in.
Ride, payment and payout recordsRecords of completed rides, payments and payouts are kept in anonymised form (with your name and phone number removed) where needed for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) regulatory purposes. Our Privacy Policy states a retention period of 7 years for these records.
Card and bank detailsCard and bank details are held by Stripe, not by Dollor.ai, and are handled under Stripe's privacy policy.
Driver background checkThe check is run by Checkr and handled under Checkr's privacy policy. Contact Checkr about data it holds.
Messages sent to other usersMessages you sent to a rider or driver as part of a ride may remain visible to that other person.

Deleting an account cannot be undone. You cannot delete your account while you have a ride in progress or an unpaid payout: finish or cancel it first. For an unresolved payment issue, contact support@dollor.ai first.

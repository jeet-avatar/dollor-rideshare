Driver Partner Agreement

Terms for Independent Drivers on the Dollor.ai Rideshare Marketplace

Effective Date: October 29, 2026
Version: 3.0
Important: This agreement governs your relationship with Dollor.ai as an independent contractor. It applies together with the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Please read carefully before you go online.

1. Nature of Relationship

1.1 Independent Contractor Status

You are an independent contractor, NOT an employee of Dollor.ai or Zietra Technologies Inc. This means:

  • You decide when you go online and when you go offline (you must finish a ride in progress before going offline)
  • You decide which ride requests to bid on and whether to bid at all
  • You may work for other platforms
  • You are responsible for your own taxes, insurance, vehicle expenses, and any license or permit your work requires
  • Dollor.ai does not guarantee any minimum earnings, hours, or number of rides, and does not provide benefits

1.2 Platform Role

Dollor.ai operates a peer-to-peer rideshare marketplace in California. Riders post a ride offer, and you may bid on it. Dollor.ai provides the technology and processes payments; the ride itself is provided by you. Dollor.ai does not provide vehicles, set the fare for you, or supervise how you drive. Dollor.ai's application for a CPUC TNC permit is under CPUC review.

2. How You Are Paid

How it works: You name your bid. When the Rider accepts, the Rider's card is authorized, and when the ride is completed the fare is captured. A flat Connection Fee and a CPUC charge are deducted from your payout. Tips are not reduced by any fee.

2.1 Connection Fee

The Connection Fee is a flat amount based on the fare, not a percentage. It is charged to you and, separately, to the Rider:

Fare Connection Fee charged to you
$35.00 or less $1.00
Over $35.00 up to $70.00 $2.00
Over $70.00 $3.00

2.2 CPUC Access for All Charge

A $0.05 California Public Utilities Commission Access for All charge applies to you, and a separate $0.05 applies to the Rider, on each completed trip.

2.3 Tips and Payouts

  • 100% of tips go to you. No fee is taken from tips.
  • Payouts are made through Stripe. You must complete Stripe's payout onboarding (bank details are entered on Stripe's page, not stored by Dollor.ai) to receive payouts.
  • The bid sheet shows your estimated earnings after fees before you submit a bid.

2.4 Cancellations and No-Shows

  • If you cancel a ride after a match, the Rider is not charged, the Rider's card authorization is released, and the request is re-opened to other drivers. Dollor.ai tracks your cancellation rate and warns you when it is high; frequent cancellations may affect your standing.
  • If the Rider cancels before the trip starts, no fee is charged to the Rider.
  • If you arrive at pickup, wait at least 5 minutes, and the Rider does not show up, you may mark the Rider as a no-show. The Rider's saved card is charged a $5.00 no-show fee and $4.00 (80%) is paid to you, if you have completed Stripe payout onboarding and the Rider's card charge succeeds.

3. Requirements

3.1 Eligibility

  • Be at least 21 years old (enforced at registration and approval)
  • Hold a valid driver's license and upload a photo of it
  • Maintain valid vehicle insurance and upload proof of it
  • Upload a profile photo
  • Provide your vehicle information
  • Complete a background check through Checkr, and be approved by Dollor.ai

You cannot go online until your account is approved and your background check has cleared. The Platform blocks going online until both are complete.

3.2 Ongoing Obligations

  • Maintain a valid license, registration, and insurance at all times
  • Keep your vehicle in safe operating condition
  • Complete rides professionally and follow all applicable laws
  • Keep your documents and information up to date

4. Conduct Standards

4.1 You Agree To:

  • Treat Riders and others with respect
  • Arrive promptly for pickups
  • Communicate professionally through the app
  • Protect Riders' personal information and use it only for the ride

4.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following may result in suspension or deactivation:

  • Harassment or discrimination of any kind
  • Operating under the influence of drugs or alcohol
  • Unsafe driving practices
  • Fraud or theft
  • Sharing or misusing Rider information

5. Insurance & Liability

5.1 Your Insurance and Licensing

You are responsible for maintaining the insurance that California law and the CPUC require of a driver providing rides arranged through an online platform, including personal auto insurance that covers this activity, and for any license or permit your work requires. Dollor.ai does not provide insurance coverage to you through this agreement. Confirm with your insurer that your policy covers rideshare activity.

5.2 Liability

As an independent contractor, you are responsible for:

  • Your own actions while providing rides
  • Damage to your vehicle
  • Traffic violations and parking tickets
  • Any claims arising from your negligence

6. Account Suspension and Deactivation

6.1 Grounds

  • Violation of these terms
  • A background check that does not clear, or a failed re-check
  • Lapse of your license or insurance
  • Safety concerns
  • Fraudulent activity

6.2 Appeal Process

If your account is deactivated, you may appeal by contacting support@dollor.ai within 30 days. We will review your case and respond within 10 business days.

7. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in accordance with the American Arbitration Association rules. You waive the right to participate in class action lawsuits.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your driver account in the driver app (Profile > Delete Account) or by emailing support@dollor.ai from your registered email address. See www.dollor.ai/delete-account.

9. Changes to Terms

We may update these terms at any time. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the new terms. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification.

Dollor Driver is the driver app for the Dollor ride marketplace. Go online, see ride requests near you, and bid the fare you want to drive for. The rider can accept your bid, counter it, or decline.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign up and submit your driver's license and vehicle insurance.
2. Complete the background check requested during setup (run by our screening partner, Checkr).
3. Once you are approved, go online whenever you want to drive.
4. Review ride requests near you, with pickup, destination, distance and the estimated fare.
5. Submit a bid or answer a counter-offer. Riders choose during a 5-minute bidding window.
6. Navigate to the pickup, complete the trip, and rate your rider.

WHAT YOU PAY AND WHAT YOU KEEP
- You keep the fare you agree on, minus a flat connection fee: $1 for fares up to $35, $2 for fares from $35 to $70, and $3 above $70.
- A $0.05 California Public Utilities Commission Access for All charge is also deducted per ride.
- There is no percentage commission.
- 100% of rider tips go to you.
- The bid screen shows your estimated earnings after fees before you submit.

FEATURES
- Online and offline switch: you choose when to drive
- Nearby ride requests with fare estimate and distance
- Bid, counter-offer, accept or decline
- Directions through Google Maps
- In-app chat with your rider, with voice dictation for hands-free messages
- Earnings summary and payout history
- Payouts through Stripe to your bank account
- Accessibility setting to receive requests that need extra space for a mobility device

REQUIREMENTS
- 21 years or older
- Valid driver's license
- Vehicle with valid personal auto insurance
- iPhone with GPS
- Background check completed through Checkr and approval before you can go online

Location: the app uses your location, including in the background while you are online, to show you nearby requests and to share your position with your rider during a trip.

You are an independent contractor, not an employee of Dollor. You are responsible for your own taxes, insurance and vehicle expenses, and you may use other platforms at the same time. Service area: California.

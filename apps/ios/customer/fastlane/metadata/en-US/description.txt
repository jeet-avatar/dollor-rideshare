Dollor is California's first open-bidding rideshare platform — where YOU negotiate the fare. Browse competing bids from nearby independent drivers, counter-offer, and choose the price that works for you.

HOW IT WORKS

Open Bidding Marketplace:
- Enter your pickup and dropoff location
- Nearby drivers submit fare bids in real time
- Accept a bid, make a counter-offer, or wait for a better price
- See the full fare breakdown before confirming — no surprises

Transparent Pricing:
- All fees shown upfront before you book
- Every fare shows its breakdown before you confirm
- Tips are 100% optional and go directly to your driver

CALIFORNIA-LICENSED TNC

Dollor operates as a licensed California Transportation Network Company (TNC) under CPUC regulations:
- All drivers pass criminal background checks (California DOJ + FBI) and annual DMV record checks
- $1,000,000 primary insurance active from the moment your driver accepts your trip (through dropoff)
- Zero tolerance policy for drug and alcohol use — violations reported immediately to CPUC
- In-app emergency SOS button connects directly to 911

SAFETY BUILT IN

- Real-time trip tracking you can share with trusted contacts
- Two-way rating system keeps drivers and riders accountable
- Driver's photo, name, vehicle make, color, and license plate shown before pickup
- 24/7 in-app incident reporting

DRIVER-PARTNERS YOU CAN TRUST

All Dollor drivers are independent contractors under California Proposition 22:
- Verified identity, valid CA driver's license, and vehicle inspection
- Freedom to set their own schedule and accept only rides they want
- Guaranteed earnings protection per Proposition 22

FOUNDING MEMBER PROGRAM

Early access members get priority ride matching and locked-in platform fees. One-time fee grants access to Dollor's real-world transportation platform (exempt from Apple In-App Purchase requirements per App Store Review Guideline 3.1.3a).

Dollor is based in Rancho Santa Margarita, California. We believe riders deserve transparent pricing and drivers deserve fair pay.
